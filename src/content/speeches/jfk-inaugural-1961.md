<section class="para">
  <span class="no">02</span>
  <div class="en">We <span class="voc"><span class="w">observe</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /əbˈzɜːrv/ 庆祝；举行（仪式）；观察</span></span> today not a victory of party but a celebration of freedom, symbolizing an end as well as a beginning, <span class="voc"><span class="w">signifying</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ˈsɪɡnɪfaɪɪŋ/ 表示，意味着</span></span> <span class="voc"><span class="w">renewal</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /rɪˈnuːəl/ 更新，复兴，重新开始</span></span> as well as change. For I have sworn before you and <span class="voc"><span class="w">Almighty</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ɔːlˈmaɪti/ 全能的（Almighty God 即全能的上帝）</span></span> God the same <span class="voc"><span class="w">solemn</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈsɑːləm/ 庄严的，郑重的</span></span> <span class="voc"><span class="w">oath</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /oʊθ/ 誓言，誓词</span></span> our <span class="voc"><span class="w">forbears</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈfɔːrberz/ 祖先，先辈（同 forebears）</span></span> <span class="voc"><span class="w">prescribed</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /prɪˈskraɪbd/ 规定，订定</span></span> nearly a century and three-quarters ago.</div>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">03</span>
  <div class="en">The world is very different now. For man holds in his <span class="voc"><span class="w">mortal</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈmɔːrtl/ 凡人的，终有一死的</span></span> hands the power to <span class="voc"><span class="w">abolish</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /əˈbɑːlɪʃ/ 废除，消除</span></span> all forms of human poverty and all forms of human life.   <div class="zh">如今的世界已大不相同。因为人类手中掌握着一种力量，既能消除一切形式的人类贫困，也能消灭一切形式的人类生命。然而，我们先辈为之奋斗的那些革命信念，在全球各地依然处于争论之中——那就是这样一种信念：人的权利并非来自国家的恩赐，而是来自上帝之手。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">04</span>
  <div class="en">We dare not forget today that we are the <span class="voc"><span class="w">heirs</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /erz/ 继承人，继承者</span></span> of that first revolution.   <div class="zh">今天我们不可忘记，我们是那场最初革命的继承者。让这句话从此时此地传向四方，无论敌友：火炬已经传递给新一代美国人——他们出生在本世纪，受过战火的淬炼，受过艰难而苦涩的和平的约束，为我们古老的传统而自豪，并且不愿目睹或容许那些人权被缓慢地蚕食——这个国家始终致力于捍卫这些人权，今天我们仍在国内和全世界为之奋斗。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">05</span>
  <div class="en">Let every nation know, whether it wishes us well or ill, that we shall pay any price, <span class="voc"><span class="w">bear</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ber/ 承担，承受</span></span> any <span class="voc"><span class="w">burden</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈbɜːrdn/ 重担，负担</span></span>, meet any <span class="voc"><span class="w">hardship</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈhɑːrdʃɪp/ 艰难困苦</span></span>, support any friend, oppose any foe to <span class="voc"><span class="w">assure</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /əˈʃʊr/ 确保，保证</span></span> the survival and the success of <span class="voc"><span class="w">liberty</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈlɪbərti/ 自由</span></span>.</div>
  <div class="zh">让每一个国家都知道，无论它对我们心怀善意还是恶意：我们将不惜任何代价，承担任何重担，应对任何艰难，支持任何朋友，反对任何敌人，以确保自由的存续与成功。</div>
</section>

  <span class="no">06</span>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">07</span>
  <div class="en">To those old <span class="voc"><span class="w">allies</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈælaɪz/ 盟友，同盟国</span></span> whose cultural and spiritual origins we share, we pledge the <span class="voc"><span class="w">loyalty</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈlɔɪəlti/ 忠诚，忠贞</span></span> of faithful friends. United there is little we cannot do in a host of cooperative <span class="voc"><span class="w">ventures</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈventʃərz/ 事业，冒险事业</span></span>. Divided there is little we can do; for we dare not meet a powerful challenge <span class="voc"><span class="w">at odds</span><span class="g"><i>phr.</i> /ət ɑːdz/ 不和，争执，意见相左</span></span> and split <span class="voc"><span class="w">asunder</span><span class="g"><i>adv.</i> /əˈsʌndər/ 分开地，四分五裂地</span></span>.</div>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">08</span>
  <div class="en">To those new states whom we welcome to the ranks of the free, we pledge our word that one form of <span class="voc"><span class="w">colonial</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /kəˈloʊniəl/ 殖民的，殖民地的</span></span> control shall not have passed away merely to be replaced by a far more <span class="voc"><span class="w">iron</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈaɪərn/ 铁的；强硬的，严酷的</span></span> <span class="voc"><span class="w">tyranny</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈtɪrəni/ 暴政，专制统治</span></span>. But we shall always hope to find them strongly supporting their own freedom; and to remember that, in the past, those who <span class="voc"><span class="w">foolishly</span><span class="g"><i>adv.</i> /ˈfuːlɪʃli/ 愚蠢地</span></span> sought power by <span class="voc"><span class="w">riding the back of the tiger</span><span class="g"><i>phr.</i> /ˈraɪdɪŋ ðə bæk əv ðə ˈtaɪɡər/ 骑虎（喻借助危险力量谋利）</span></span> ended up inside.</div>
  <div class="zh">对于那些我们欢迎加入自由行列的新兴国家，我们郑重承诺：一种殖民统治的消亡，不应仅仅被一种更为严酷的铁腕暴政所取代。我们不会总是指望它们支持我们的立场，但我们将始终希望它们坚定地支持自身的自由；并且记住：过去那些愚蠢地骑在虎背上求取权力的人，最终都落入了虎口。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">09</span>
  <div class="en">To those people in the <span class="voc"><span class="w">huts</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /hʌts/ 小屋，棚屋</span></span> and villages of half the globe struggling to break the <span class="voc"><span class="w">bonds</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /bɑːndz/ 束缚，枷锁</span></span> of <span class="voc"><span class="w">mass</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /mæs/ 大规模的，大众的</span></span> <span class="voc"><span class="w">misery</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈmɪzəri/ 苦难，贫困</span></span>, we pledge our best efforts to help them help themselves, for whatever period is required–not because the <span class="voc"><span class="w">communists</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈkɑːmjənɪsts/ 共产党人</span></span> may be doing it, not because we seek their votes, but because it is right.   <div class="zh">对于生活在半个地球的茅屋与村落中、正奋力挣脱大众贫困枷锁的人民，我们承诺竭尽全力帮助他们自救，无论需要多长时间——不是因为共产党人可能这样做，不是因为我们想要他们的选票，而是因为这样做是正确的。如果一个自由社会不能帮助众多的穷人，它也就无法保全少数富人。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">10</span>
  <div class="en">To our sister <span class="voc"><span class="w">republics</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /rɪˈpʌblɪks/ 共和国</span></span> south of our border, we offer a special pledge: to convert our good words into good deeds in a new <span class="voc"><span class="w">alliance</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /əˈlaɪəns/ 联盟，同盟</span></span> for progress; to assist free men and free governments in <span class="voc"><span class="w">casting off</span><span class="g"><i>phr.</i> /ˈkæstɪŋ ɔːf/ 摆脱，抛弃</span></span> the chains of poverty.
