<section class="para">
  <span class="no">02</span>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">03</span>
  <div class="en">Greece is not a rich country.   <div class="zh">希腊并非富裕之国。自然资源的匮乏，一向迫使希腊人民辛勤劳作以勉强糊口。自 1940 年以来，这个勤劳而爱好和平的国家先后遭受入侵、四年残酷的敌国占领，以及痛苦的内乱。</div>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">04</span>
  <div class="en">When forces of <span class="voc"><span class="w">liberation</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˌlɪbəˈreɪʃn/ 解放</span></span> entered Greece they found that the <span class="voc"><span class="w">retreating</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /rɪˈtriːtɪŋ/ 撤退中的</span></span> Germans had destroyed <span class="voc"><span class="w">virtually</span><span class="g"><i>adv.</i> /ˈvɜːrtʃuəli/ 几乎，实际上</span></span> all the railways, roads, port facilities, communications, and <span class="voc"><span class="w">merchant marine</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˌmɜːrtʃənt məˈriːn/ 商船队</span></span>. Eighty-five percent of the children were <span class="voc"><span class="w">tubercular</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /tuˈbɜːrkjələr/ 患结核病的</span></span>. <span class="voc"><span class="w">Livestock</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈlaɪvstɑːk/ 牲畜，家畜</span></span>, poultry, and draft animals had almost disappeared. <span class="voc"><span class="w">Inflation</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ɪnˈfleɪʃn/ 通货膨胀</span></span> had wiped out practically all <span class="voc"><span class="w">savings</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈseɪvɪŋz/ 储蓄，存款</span></span>.</div>
  <div class="zh">当解放部队进入希腊时，他们发现撤退中的德军几乎摧毁了所有的铁路、公路、港口设施、通讯系统与商船队。一千多个村庄被焚毁。百分之八十五的儿童患有结核病。牲畜、家禽与役畜几乎绝迹。通货膨胀吞噬了几乎所有的储蓄。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">05</span>
  <div class="en">As a result of these tragic conditions, a <span class="voc"><span class="w">militant</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈmɪlɪtənt/ 好战的，激进的</span></span> <span class="voc"><span class="w">minority</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /maɪˈnɔːrəti/ 少数派，少数</span></span>, <span class="voc"><span class="w">exploiting</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ɪkˈsplɔɪtɪŋ/ 利用，榨取</span></span> human <span class="voc"><span class="w">want</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /wɑːnt/ 匮乏，贫困</span></span> and misery, was able to create political <span class="voc"><span class="w">chaos</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈkeɪɑːs/ 混乱，动荡</span></span> which, until now, has made economic recovery impossible.</div>
  <div class="zh">由于这些悲惨的状况，一个激进的少数派利用人们的匮乏与苦难，制造出政治混乱，以致时至今日经济复苏仍无从谈起。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">06</span>
  <div class="en">Greece is today without funds to finance the <span class="voc"><span class="w">importation</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˌɪmpɔːrˈteɪʃn/ 进口，输入</span></span> of those goods which are essential to bare <span class="voc"><span class="w">subsistence</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /səbˈsɪstəns/ 生存，最低生活维持</span></span>. Greece is in <span class="voc"><span class="w">desperate</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈdespərət/ 极度需要的；绝望的</span></span> need of financial and economic assistance to enable it to <span class="voc"><span class="w">resume</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /rɪˈzuːm/ 恢复，重新开始</span></span> purchases of food, clothing, fuel, and seeds.   <div class="zh">今天的希腊没有资金进口那些维持最低生存所必需的物资。在这种情况下，希腊人民无法在解决重建问题上取得进展。希腊迫切需要财政与经济援助，以重新购买粮食、衣物、燃料和种子。这些物资为其人民维持生计所不可或缺，且只能从国外获得。希腊必须得到帮助，以进口恢复国内秩序与安全所必需的物资——而这对于经济与政治复苏至关重要。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">07</span>
  <div class="en">The Greek Government has also asked for the assistance of experienced American <span class="voc"><span class="w">administrators</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ədˈmɪnɪstreɪtərz/ 行政管理人员</span></span>, economists, and <span class="voc"><span class="w">technicians</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /tekˈnɪʃnz/ 技术人员</span></span> to <span class="voc"><span class="w">insure</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ɪnˈʃʊr/ 确保，保证</span></span> that the financial and other aid given to Greece shall be used effectively in creating a stable and <span class="voc"><span class="w">self-sustaining</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˌself səˈsteɪnɪŋ/ 自给自足的，能自我维持的</span></span> economy and in improving its <span class="voc"><span class="w">public administration</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˌpʌblɪk ədˌmɪnɪˈstreɪʃn/ 公共行政</span></span>.</div>
  <div class="zh">希腊政府还请求派遣有经验的美国行政人员、经济学家与技术人员，以确保给予希腊的财政及其他援助能够得到有效使用，从而建立起稳定且能自我维持的经济，并改善其公共行政。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">08</span>
  <div class="en">The very <span class="voc"><span class="w">existence</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ɪɡˈzɪstəns/ 存在，存续</span></span> of the Greek state is today threatened by the <span class="voc"><span class="w">terrorist</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈterərɪst/ 恐怖主义的</span></span> activities of several thousand armed men, led by Communists, who <span class="voc"><span class="w">defy</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /dɪˈfaɪ/ 公然违抗，藐视</span></span> the Government's authority at a number of points, particularly along the northern <span class="voc"><span class="w">boundaries</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈbaʊndriz/ 边界，边境</span></span>.   <div class="zh">今天，希腊国家的存亡本身正受到数千名由共产党人领导的武装分子恐怖活动的威胁，他们在若干地点、尤其是北部边界一带公然对抗政府权威。联合国安全理事会任命的一个委员会目前正在调查希腊北部的动荡局势，以及据称发生在希腊与阿尔巴尼亚、保加利亚和南斯拉夫之间边界的越界行为。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">09</span>
  <div class="en">Meanwhile, the Greek Government is unable to <span class="voc"><span class="w">cope with</span><span class="g"><i>phr.</i> /ˈkoʊp wɪð/ 应对，应付</span></span> the situation. The Greek Army is small and poorly <span class="voc"><span class="w">equipped</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ɪˈkwɪpt/ 装备的，配备的</span></span>. It needs <span class="voc"><span class="w">supplies</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /səˈplaɪz/ 补给品，物资</span></span> and equipment if it is to restore the authority of the Government throughout Greek <span class="voc"><span class="w">territory</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈterətɔːri/ 领土，版图</span></span>. <section class="para">
  <span class="no">10</span>
  <div class="en">The United States must supply this assistance. We have already <span class="voc"><span class="w">extended</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ɪkˈstendɪd/ 给予，提供</span></span> to Greece certain types of <span class="voc"><span class="w">relief</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /rɪˈliːf/ 救济，救援</span></span> and economic aid but these are <span class="voc"><span class="w">inadequate</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ɪnˈædɪkwət/ 不足的，不够的</span></span>.   <div class="zh">美国必须提供这种援助。我们已向希腊提供了某些类型的救济与经济援助，但这些远远不够。民主的希腊没有别的国家可以求助。没有其他国家愿意并且能够为一个民主的希腊政府提供必要的支持。</div>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">12</span>

<section class="para">

</section>

<section class="para">
