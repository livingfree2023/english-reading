<section class="para">
  <span class="no">01</span>
  <div class="en">Mr. Vice President, Mr. Speaker, members of the Senate and the House of Representatives: Yesterday, December 7th, 1941—a date which will live in <span class="voc"><span class="w">infamy</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈɪnfəmi/ 声名狼藉；耻辱</span></span>—the United States of America was suddenly and <span class="voc"><span class="w">deliberately</span><span class="g"><i>adv.</i> /dɪˈlɪbərətli/ 蓄意地，故意地</span></span> attacked by <span class="voc"><span class="w">naval</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈneɪvl/ 海军的</span></span> and air forces of the <span class="voc"><span class="w">Empire</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈempaɪər/ 帝国</span></span> of Japan.</div>

</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">02</span>
  <div class="en">The United States was at peace with that nation, and, at the <span class="voc"><span class="w">solicitation</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /səˌlɪsɪˈteɪʃn/ 请求，恳求；劝诱</span></span> of Japan, was still in conversation with its Government and its Emperor looking toward the <span class="voc"><span class="w">maintenance</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈmeɪntənəns/ 维持，保持</span></span> of peace in the Pacific.   <div class="zh">美国当时与那个国家处于和平状态，并且应日本之请，仍在与其政府和天皇进行对话，以期维持太平洋地区的和平。事实上，在日本空军机群开始轰炸美国瓦胡岛一小时之后，日本驻美国大使及其同僚才向我国国务卿递交了对最近一份美国照会的正式答复。而这份答复虽然声称继续现有的外交谈判已无意义，却丝毫没有提及战争或武装进攻的威胁或暗示。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">03</span>
  <div class="en">It will be <span class="voc"><span class="w">recorded</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /rɪˈkɔːrdɪd/ 记载，记录</span></span> that the distance of Hawaii from Japan makes it <span class="voc"><span class="w">obvious</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈɑːbviəs/ 明显的，显而易见的</span></span> that the attack was deliberately planned many days or even weeks ago.   <div class="zh">必须记录在案的是：夏威夷与日本相距遥远，这就使显而易见——此次攻击是许多天甚至数周之前就已蓄意策划的。在这段时间里，日本政府蓄意以虚假的声明和希望继续和平的表示来欺骗美国。</div>
</section>
