### 01

Over a century ago Washington laid the [[corner stone|n.|/ˈkɔːrnər stoʊn/|奠基石]] of the Capitol in what was then little more than a [[tract|n.|/trækt/|一片土地，地带]] of wooded [[wilderness|n.|/ˈwɪldərnəs/|荒野，荒地]] here beside the Potomac. We now find it necessary to provide by great additional buildings for the business of the government.

> 一个多世纪以前，华盛顿在波托马克河畔这片当时不过是林木覆盖的荒地上，为国会大厦奠定了基石。如今我们发现，必须靠大规模增建楼宇，才能容纳政府的公务。

### 02

This growth in the need for the housing of the government is but a proof and example of the way in which the nation has grown and the [[sphere|n.|/sfɪr/|范围，领域]] of action of the national government has grown. We now [[administer|v.|/ədˈmɪnɪstər/|管理，治理]] the affairs of a nation in which the extraordinary growth of population has been [[outstripped|v.|/ˌaʊtˈstrɪpt/|被超过，被胜过]] by the growth of wealth in complex interests. The material problems that face us today are not such as they were in Washington's time, but the [[underlying|adj.|/ˌʌndərˈlaɪɪŋ/|根本的，潜在的]] facts of human nature are the same now as they were then. Under altered external form we war with the same [[tendencies|n.|/ˈtendənsiz/|倾向，趋势]] toward evil that were evident in Washington's time, and are helped by the same tendencies for good. It is about some of these that I wish to say a word today.

> 政府办公场所需求的这种增长，恰恰证明了并且例示了国家是如何成长的、国家政府的行动范围又是如何扩大的。我们今天治理的这个国家，人口的空前增长已被错综利益中财富的增长所超越。我们今天面对的物质问题已不同于华盛顿时代的问题，但人性的根本事实，今昔并无二致。在外在形式改变之后，我们仍在与华盛顿时代显而易见的那同一些向恶的倾向搏斗，也仍受那同一些向善的倾向扶助。今天我想就其中几点说几句话。

### 03

In Bunyan's “Pilgrim's Progress” you may [[recall|v.|/rɪˈkɔːl/|回想起]] the description of the Man with the Muck Rake, the man who could look no way but downward, with the muck [[rake|n.|/reɪk/|耙子]] in his hand; who was offered a [[celestial|adj.|/səˈlestʃəl/|天上的，神圣的]] crown for his muck rake, but who would neither look up nor regard the crown he was offered, but continued to rake to himself the [[filth|n.|/fɪlθ/|污秽，脏物]] of the floor.

> 在班扬的《天路历程》里，你或许还记得那个“拿着粪耙的人”的描写：他除了向下看，别处都看不了，手里握着粪耙；有人要用一顶天国的冠冕换他的粪耙，他却既不肯抬头，也不看那递来的冠冕，只顾继续把地上的污秽耙到自己身边。

### 04

In “Pilgrim's Progress” the Man with the Muck Rake is set forth as the example of him whose vision is fixed on [[carnal|adj.|/ˈkɑːrnl/|肉欲的，世俗的]] instead of spiritual things. Yet he also [[typifies|v.|/ˈtɪpɪfaɪz/|是……的典型，代表]] the man who in this life consistently refuses to see [[aught|pron.|/ɔːt/|任何事物（古语）]] that is [[lofty|adj.|/ˈlɔːfti/|高尚的，崇高的]], and fixes his eyes with [[solemn|adj.|/ˈsɑːləm/|严肃的，郑重的]] intentness only on that which is [[vile|adj.|/vaɪl/|卑劣的，丑恶的]] and [[debasing|adj.|/dɪˈbeɪsɪŋ/|使人堕落的]].

> 在《天路历程》中，“拿着粪耙的人”被举为那种心思专注于肉体而非属灵之事者的典型。但他同时也代表这样一种人：在此生中一味拒绝去看任何高尚之物，只以郑重的专注把目光钉在卑劣堕落之事上。

### 05

Now, it is very necessary that we should not [[flinch|v.|/flɪntʃ/|退缩，畏避]] from seeing what is vile and debasing. There is filth on the floor, and it must be [[scraped|v.|/skreɪpt/|刮起，铲除]] up with the muck rake; and there are times and places where this service is the most needed of all the services that can be performed. But the man who never does anything else, who never thinks or speaks or writes, [[save|prep.|/seɪv/|除……之外（古语）]] of his feats with the muck rake, [[speedily|adv.|/ˈspiːdɪli/|迅速地]] becomes, not a help but one of the most [[potent|adj.|/ˈpoʊtnt/|强有力的]] forces for evil.

> 现在，我们绝不能畏缩着不去看那些卑劣堕落之事，这一点极为必要。地上确有污秽，必须用粪耙把它铲起来；而在某些时候、某些场合，这项服务是一切可做之事中最被需要的。但是，若一个人除此之外什么都不做，除了炫耀自己用粪耙的功绩之外从不思考、从不说话、从不写作，那么他很快就会不再是助力，而成为最有力的作恶势力之一。

### 06

There are in the [[body politic|phr.|/ˈbɑːdi ˈpɑːlətɪk/|政治机体，国家]], economic and social, many and [[grave|adj.|/ɡreɪv/|严重的]] evils, and there is urgent necessity for the [[sternest|adj.|/ˈstɜːrnɪst/|最严厉的，最坚决的]] war upon them. There should be [[relentless|adj.|/rɪˈlentləs/|毫不留情的]] exposure of and attack upon every evil man, whether politician or business man, every evil practice, whether in politics, business, or social life. I hail as a [[benefactor|n.|/ˈbenɪfæktər/|恩人，造福者]] every writer or speaker, every man who, on the platform or in a book, magazine, or newspaper, with [[merciless|adj.|/ˈmɜːrsɪləs/|无情的]] [[severity|n.|/sɪˈverəti/|严厉，严酷]] makes such attack, provided always that he in his turn remembers that the attack is of use only if it is absolutely truthful.

> 在政治、经济与社会这个机体中，存在着许多严重的弊端，亟需对它们发起最严厉的战争。对于每一个恶人——无论是政客还是商人——每一种恶行——无论是在政治、商业还是社会生活中——都应当予以毫不留情的揭露和攻击。我把每一位作家、每一位演说家都视为造福者；凡是站在讲台上，或是在书本、杂志、报纸上，以无情的严厉发起这种攻击的人，我都视其为造福者——前提是他自己始终记得：这种攻击唯有绝对真实，才有用处。

### 07

The liar is no whit better than the thief, and if his [[mendacity|n.|/menˈdæsəti/|说谎，虚伪]] takes the form of [[slander|n.|/ˈslændər/|诽谤，造谣中伤]] he may be worse than most thieves. It puts a [[premium|n.|/ˈpriːmiəm/|奖励，助长]] upon [[knavery|n.|/ˈneɪvəri/|欺诈，无赖行径]] untruthfully to attack an honest man, or even with [[hysterical|adj.|/hɪˈsterɪkl/|歇斯底里的]] exaggeration to [[assail|v.|/əˈseɪl/|攻击，抨击]] a bad man with untruth.

> 说谎者一点也不比窃贼好；而倘若他的谎言采取诽谤的形式，他可能比大多数窃贼更坏。以不实之词攻击一个诚实的人，甚至以歇斯底里的夸张用谎言去抨击一个坏人，这都是在为无赖行径提供奖赏。

### 08

An [[epidemic|n.|/ˌepɪˈdemɪk/|流行，泛滥]] of [[indiscriminate|adj.|/ˌɪndɪˈskrɪmɪnət/|不分青红皂白的]] assault upon character does no good, but very great harm. The soul of every [[scoundrel|n.|/ˈskaʊndrəl/|恶棍，无赖]] is [[gladdened|v.|/ˈɡlædnd/|使高兴，使欣慰]] whenever an honest man is assailed, or even when a scoundrel is untruthfully assailed.

> 对人格不加区分地攻击之风一旦流行，不但无益，而且为害极大。每当一个诚实的人受到攻击，甚至当一个恶棍受到不实攻击时，每一个恶棍的心里都会暗自高兴。

### 09

Now, it is easy to [[twist out of shape|phr.|/twɪst aʊt əv ʃeɪp/|曲解，歪曲]] what I have just said, easy to [[affect|v.|/əˈfekt/|假装，装作]] to misunderstand it, and if it is [[slurred over|phr.|/slɜːrd ˈoʊvər/|一带而过，轻描淡写]] in repetition not difficult really to misunderstand it. Some persons are sincerely incapable of understanding that to [[denounce|v.|/dɪˈnaʊns/|谴责，公开指责]] mud slinging does not mean the [[endorsement|n.|/ɪnˈdɔːrsmənt/|认可，背书]] of [[whitewashing|n.|/ˈwaɪtwɑːʃɪŋ/|洗白，粉饰]]; and both the interested individuals who need whitewashing and those others who practice mud slinging like to encourage such confusion of ideas.

> 现在，把我刚才所说的话加以曲解是容易的，假装听不懂也是容易的；而如果转述时一带而过，那就不难真正把它误解了。有些人确实无法理解：谴责泼污抹黑，并不等于为粉饰洗白背书；而那些需要被洗白的利害相关者，以及那些惯于泼污抹黑的人，都喜欢助长这种观念上的混淆。

### 10

One of the chief [[counts|n.|/kaʊnts/|罪状，指控]] against those who make indiscriminate assault upon men in business or men in public life is that they [[invite|v.|/ɪnˈvaɪt/|招致，引来]] a reaction which is sure to tell powerfully in favor of the [[unscrupulous|adj.|/ʌnˈskruːpjələs/|肆无忌惮的，不择手段的]] scoundrel who really ought to be attacked, who ought to be exposed, who ought, if possible, to be put in the [[penitentiary|n.|/ˌpenɪˈtenʃəri/|监狱，感化院]]. If Aristides is praised overmuch as just, people get tired of hearing it; and [[overcensure|n.|/ˌoʊvərˈsenʃər/|过分指责]] of the unjust finally and from similar reasons results in their favor.

> 对那些不加区分地攻击商界人士或公职人员的人，主要罪状之一是：他们招致一种反作用，而这种反作用必定会大大有利于那些真正该被攻击、该被揭露、该在可能时被送进监狱的肆无忌惮的恶棍。如果阿里斯提德被过分地赞为公正，人们听久了也会厌烦；而对不义者的过度指责，最终也会出于类似的道理反而对他们有利。

### 11

Any [[excess|n.|/ɪkˈses/|过度，过分]] is almost sure to invite a reaction; and, unfortunately, the reactions instead of taking the form of punishment of those guilty of the excess, is apt to take the form either of punishment of the unoffending or of giving [[immunity|n.|/ɪˈmjuːnəti/|豁免，免受追究]], and even strength, to offenders. The effort to make financial or political profit out of the destruction of character can only result in public [[calamity|n.|/kəˈlæməti/|灾难，祸患]]. Gross and [[reckless|adj.|/ˈrekləs/|鲁莽的，不顾后果的]] assaults on character, whether on the stump or in newspaper, magazine, or book, create a [[morbid|adj.|/ˈmɔːrbɪd/|病态的]] and [[vicious|adj.|/ˈvɪʃəs/|恶毒的，堕落的]] public sentiment, and at the same time act as a profound [[deterrent|n.|/dɪˈtɜːrənt/|威慑，阻遏因素]] to able men of normal [[sensitiveness|n.|/ˈsensətɪvnəs/|敏感，易受触动]] and tend to prevent them from entering the public service at any price.

> 任何过度之举几乎都必定招致反作用；而不幸的是，这种反作用往往不是去惩罚那些过度之举的罪魁，反而要么惩罚无辜者，要么给予违法者豁免，甚至助长其势力。企图从毁坏他人名声中谋取金钱或政治利益，只能导致公共灾难。粗暴而轻率的攻讦——无论是在竞选讲台上，还是在报纸、杂志或书本中——都会制造一种病态而恶毒的公共情绪，同时也构成一种深重的威慑，使那些感觉正常的能干之士望而却步，无论出价多高都不愿进入公职。

### 12

As an instance in point, I may mention that one serious difficulty [[encountered|v.|/ɪnˈkaʊntərd/|遇到，遭遇]] in getting the right type of men to dig the Panama canal is the certainty that they will be exposed, both without, and, I am sorry to say, sometimes within, Congress, to utterly reckless assaults on their character and [[capacity|n.|/kəˈpæsəti/|能力，才干]].

> 举一个恰切的例子：在招募合适的人去开凿巴拿马运河时，遇到的一个严重困难就是，这些人必定会在国会之外——而且我很遗憾地说，有时还在国会之内——遭受对其人格与能力的全然不顾后果的攻击。

### 13

At the risk of repetition let me say again that my plea is not for immunity to, but for the most [[unsparing|adj.|/ʌnˈsperɪŋ/|毫不留情的，严酷的]] exposure of, the politician who [[betrays|v.|/bɪˈtreɪz/|背叛，辜负]] his trust, of the big business man who makes or spends his fortune in [[illegitimate|adj.|/ˌɪlɪˈdʒɪtɪmət/|非法的，不正当的]] or corrupt ways. There should be a [[resolute|adj.|/ˈrezəluːt/|坚决的，果断的]] effort to hunt every such man out of the position he has disgraced. Expose the crime, and hunt down the criminal; but remember that even in the case of crime, if it is attacked in sensational, [[lurid|adj.|/ˈlʊrɪd/|耸人听闻的，骇人的]], and untruthful fashion, the attack may do more damage to the public mind than the crime itself.

> 我甘冒重复之嫌，再说一遍：我所呼吁的，不是对背叛职责的政客、以不正当或腐败手段赚取或挥霍财富的大商人给予豁免，而是对他们进行最不留情的揭露。应当坚决努力，把每一个这样的人从他玷污了的职位上赶出去。揭露罪行，追捕罪犯；但要记住，即便是罪行，若以耸人听闻、骇人听闻且不实的方式加以攻击，这种攻击对公众心灵造成的损害，可能比罪行本身还大。

### 14

It is because I feel that there should be no rest in the endless war against the forces of evil that I ask the war be conducted with [[sanity|n.|/ˈsænəti/|理智，清醒]] as well as with [[resolution|n.|/ˌrezəˈluːʃn/|决心，坚定]]. The men with the muck rakes are often [[indispensable|adj.|/ˌɪndɪˈspensəbl/|不可或缺的]] to the well being of society; but only if they know when to stop raking the muck, and to look upward to the celestial crown above them, to the crown of worthy [[endeavor|n.|/ɪnˈdevər/|努力，事业]]. There are beautiful things above and round about them; and if they gradually grow to feel that the whole world is nothing but muck, their power of usefulness is gone.

> 正是因为我感到，与恶势力的这场无尽之战不应有片刻停歇，所以我才要求这场战争既要有决心，也要有理智。拿着粪耙的人，往往对社会福祉不可或缺；但前提是他们知道何时该停止耙粪，抬头仰望他们上方那顶天国的冠冕——那顶值得为之努力的冠冕。他们上方与四周都有美好的事物；倘若他们渐渐觉得整个世界无非是一堆污秽，他们的可用之处也就消失了。

### 15

If the whole picture is painted black there remains no [[hue|n.|/hjuː/|色调，色彩]] whereby to single out the [[rascals|n.|/ˈræsklz/|无赖，流氓]] for distinction from their fellows. Such painting finally [[induces|v.|/ɪnˈduːsɪz/|引起，导致]] a kind of moral color blindness; and people affected by it come to the conclusion that no man is really black, and no man really white, but they are all gray.

> 如果整幅画都涂成黑色，那就再没有别的色调可以把无赖从众人中挑出来加以区分了。这样的涂抹最终会导致一种道德上的色盲；受其影响的人会得出结论说：没有谁是真正的黑，也没有谁是真正的白，大家都是灰的。

### 16

In other words, they neither believe in the truth of the attack, nor in the honesty of the man who is attacked; they grow as suspicious of the accusation as of the offense; it becomes [[well nigh|adv.|/ˌwel ˈnaɪ/|几乎，近乎]] hopeless to stir them either to [[wrath|n.|/ræθ/|愤怒，义愤]] against wrongdoing or to enthusiasm for what is right; and such a mental attitude in the public gives hope to every [[knave|n.|/neɪv/|无赖，骗子]], and is the [[despair|n.|/dɪˈsper/|绝望，令人绝望之事]] of honest men. To assail the great and admitted evils of our political and industrial life with such crude and [[sweeping|adj.|/ˈswiːpɪŋ/|笼统的，一概而论的]] generalizations as to include decent men in the general [[condemnation|n.|/ˌkɑːndemˈneɪʃn/|谴责，定罪]] means the [[searing|n.|/ˈsɪrɪŋ/|烧灼，麻木]] of the public conscience. There results a general attitude either of [[cynical|adj.|/ˈsɪnɪkl/|愤世嫉俗的，玩世不恭的]] belief in and indifference to public corruption or else of a distrustful inability to discriminate between the good and the bad. Either attitude is [[fraught|adj.|/frɔːt/|充满……的，伴随……的]] with untold damage to the country as a whole.

> 换句话说，他们既不相信攻击的真实性，也不相信被攻击者的诚实；他们对指控与对罪行同样怀疑；要激起他们对恶行的愤怒，或对正义的热忱，几乎已无望；公众中的这样一种心态，让每一个无赖都看到希望，也让诚实的人深感绝望。以如此粗糙而笼统的概括去抨击我们政治与工业生活中那些重大而公认的弊端，以致把体面人也一并纳入谴责之中，这就意味着把公众的良知烧灼麻木。其结果是一种普遍的心态：要么是对公共腐败抱着玩世不恭的相信与漠然，要么是疑虑重重、无力分辨善恶。无论哪种心态，都会给整个国家带来难以估量的损害。

### 17

The fool who has not sense to discriminate between what is good and what is bad is well nigh as dangerous as the man who does discriminate and yet chooses the bad. There is nothing more distressing to every good patriot, to every good American, than the hard, [[scoffing|adj.|/ˈskɑːfɪŋ/|嘲弄的，讥笑的]] spirit which treats the [[allegation|n.|/ˌæləˈɡeɪʃn/|指控，说法]] of dishonesty in a public man as a cause for laughter. Such laughter is worse than the [[crackling|n.|/ˈkræklɪŋ/|噼啪声]] of [[thorns|n.|/θɔːrnz/|荆棘]] under a pot, for it denotes not merely the vacant mind, but the heart in which high emotions have been choked before they could grow to [[fruition|n.|/fruˈɪʃn/|结果，成熟]]. There is any amount of good in the world, and there never was a time when loftier and more [[disinterested|adj.|/dɪsˈɪntrəstɪd/|无私的，不谋私利的]] work for the [[betterment|n.|/ˈbetərmənt/|改善，改良]] of mankind was being done than now. The forces that tend for evil are great and terrible, but the forces of truth and love and courage and honesty and generosity and sympathy are also stronger than ever before. It is a foolish and timid, no less than a wicked thing, to [[blink|v.|/blɪŋk/|无视，视而不见]] the fact that the forces of evil are strong, but it is even worse to fail to take into account the strength of the forces that tell for good.

> 那种无力分辨善恶的蠢人，几乎和那种能够分辨却偏要选择恶的人一样危险。对于一个好爱国者、一个好美国人来说，最令人痛心的，莫过于那种冷硬而讥笑的精神，把对公职人员不诚实的指控当作笑料。这样的笑比锅下荆棘的噼啪声更糟，因为它不仅表明头脑空虚，还表明那颗心在崇高的情感尚未长成结果之前就已被扼杀。世上善的东西多得不可胜数，而人类为改善自身所做的最高尚、最无私的工作，也从未有如今日之盛。向恶的势力固然强大可怖，但真理、爱、勇气、诚实、慷慨与同情的力量，也从未如今日之强。无视恶势力强大这一事实，是愚蠢怯懦之举，其恶劣不亚于作恶；然而更糟的是，不去考虑那些向善力量的分量。

### 18

Hysterical [[sensationalism|n.|/senˈseɪʃənəlɪzəm/|耸人听闻，煽情手法]] is the poorest weapon wherewith to fight for lasting [[righteousness|n.|/ˈraɪtʃəsnəs/|正义，正直]]. The men who with stern [[sobriety|n.|/səˈbraɪəti/|冷静，节制]] and truth assail the many evils of our time, whether in the public press, or in magazines, or in books, are the leaders and allies of all engaged in the work for social and political betterment. But if they give good reason for distrust of what they say, if they chill the [[ardor|n.|/ˈɑːrdər/|热情，热忱]] of those who demand truth as a primary virtue, they thereby [[betray|v.|/bɪˈtreɪ/|背叛，辜负]] the good cause and play into the hands of the very men against whom they are [[nominally|adv.|/ˈnɑːmɪnəli/|名义上]] at war.

> 歇斯底里的耸人听闻，是为持久的正义而战的最拙劣的武器。那些以严正的冷静与真实，抨击我们时代诸多弊端的人——无论是在公共报刊、杂志还是书本中——都是所有致力于社会与政治改良之人的领袖与盟友。但是，倘若他们让人有充分理由怀疑他们所说的话，倘若他们冷却了那些把真实奉为首要美德之人的热忱，他们便由此辜负了这桩善业，恰好帮了他们名义上与之作战的那些人的忙。

### 19

In his Ecclesiastical Polity that fine old Elizabethan [[divine|n.|/dɪˈvaɪn/|神学家，牧师]], Bishop Hooker, wrote:

> 那位伊丽莎白时代可敬的老神学家胡克主教，在其《教会政体》一书中写道：

### 20

He that goeth about to persuade a [[multitude|n.|/ˈmʌltɪtuːd/|大众，民众]] that they are not so well governed as they ought to be shall never want attentive and favorable hearers, because they know the [[manifold|adj.|/ˈmænɪfoʊld/|多种多样的]] defects whereunto every kind of [[regimen|n.|/ˈredʒɪmən/|政体，统治方式]] is subject, but the secret [[lets|n.|/lets/|障碍，阻碍]] and difficulties, which in public proceedings are innumerable and inevitable, they have not ordinarily the judgment to consider. This truth should be kept constantly in mind by every free people desiring to preserve the sanity and [[poise|n.|/pɔɪz/|镇定，从容]] indispensable to the permanent success of self-government. Yet, on the other hand, it is vital not to permit this spirit of sanity and self-command to degenerate into mere mental [[stagnation|n.|/stæɡˈneɪʃn/|停滞，僵化]]. Bad though a state of hysterical excitement is, and evil though the results are which come from the violent [[oscillations|n.|/ˌɑːsɪˈleɪʃnz/|摇摆，波动]] such excitement invariably produces, yet a [[sodden|adj.|/ˈsɑːdn/|麻木的，迟钝的]] [[acquiescence|n.|/ˌækwiˈesns/|默许，顺从]] in evil is even worse.

> 凡欲劝说大众相信他们所受的治理不如应有的那么好的人，永远不会缺少专注而友善的听众；因为他们知道每一种政体都难免有种种缺陷，然而公共事务中那些隐秘的阻碍与困难，繁多而不可避免，他们通常却没有判断力去加以考虑。凡欲维护自治制度永久成功所不可或缺的理智与镇定的自由民族，都应把这一真理常记于心。然而另一方面，也绝不能让这种理智与自制的精神蜕变为纯粹的心智停滞。歇斯底里的激动状态固然糟糕，这种激动必然造成的剧烈摇摆所带来的后果固然恶劣，但对恶麻木的默许却更糟。

### 21

At this moment we are passing through a period of great [[unrest|n.|/ʌnˈrest/|动荡，不安]]—social, political, and industrial unrest. It is of the utmost importance for our future that this should prove to be not the unrest of mere [[rebelliousness|n.|/rɪˈbeliəsnəs/|叛逆，反叛情绪]] against life, of mere dissatisfaction with the [[inevitable|adj.|/ɪnˈevɪtəbl/|不可避免的]] inequality of conditions, but the unrest of a [[resolute|adj.|/ˈrezəluːt/|坚定的，果决的]] and eager ambition to secure the betterment of the individual and the nation.

> 此时此刻，我们正经历一个剧烈动荡的时期——社会、政治与工业的动荡。对我们的未来而言，极其重要的是：这种动荡最终应被证明不是对生活本身的单纯反叛，不是对条件不可避免的不平等单纯的不满，而是一种坚定而热切的志向所驱动的动荡，志在实现个人与国家的改善。

### 22

So far as this movement of [[agitation|n.|/ˌædʒɪˈteɪʃn/|鼓动，骚动]] throughout the country takes the form of a fierce [[discontent|n.|/ˌdɪskənˈtent/|不满]] with evil, of a determination to punish the authors of evil, whether in industry or politics, the feeling is to be [[heartily|adv.|/ˈhɑːrtɪli/|由衷地，热诚地]] welcomed as a sign of healthy life.

> 只要这场遍及全国的鼓动运动表现为对恶的强烈不满，表现为惩治恶之始作俑者（无论其身处产业界还是政界）的决心，那么这种情绪就应当被由衷地欢迎，视之为健康生命的标志。

### 23

If, on the other hand, it turns into a mere [[crusade|n.|/kruːˈseɪd/|运动，讨伐]] of appetite against appetite, of a contest between the [[brutal|adj.|/ˈbruːtl/|野蛮的，兽性的]] [[greed|n.|/ɡriːd/|贪婪]] of the “have nots” and the brutal greed of the “haves,” then it has no significance for good, but only for evil. If it seeks to establish a line of [[cleavage|n.|/ˈkliːvɪdʒ/|分裂，裂痕]], not along the line which divides good men from bad, but along that other line, running at right angles thereto, which divides those who are well off from those who are less well off, then it will be fraught with [[immeasurable|adj.|/ɪˈmeʒərəbl/|无法估量的]] harm to the body politic.

> 反之，倘若它变成一场单纯的欲望对欲望的讨伐，变成“一无所有者”的野蛮贪婪与“有产者”的野蛮贪婪之间的争斗，那么它对善毫无意义，只对恶有意义。倘若它想要划出一道分裂的界线，不是沿着区分好人与坏人的那条线，而是沿着与之成直角的另一条线——区分富裕者与不那么富裕者的那条线——那么它就会给政治机体带来无法估量的损害。

### 24

We can no more and no less afford to [[condone|v.|/kənˈdoʊn/|宽恕，纵容]] evil in the man of [[capital|n.|/ˈkæpɪtl/|资本]] than evil in the man of no capital. The wealthy man who [[exults|v.|/ɪɡˈzʌlts/|得意，欢欣]] because there is a failure of justice in the effort to bring some trust [[magnate|n.|/ˈmæɡneɪt/|巨头，大亨]] to account for his misdeeds is as bad as, and no worse than, the so-called labor leader who [[clamorously|adv.|/ˈklæmərəsli/|喧嚷地，吵闹地]] strives to excite a foul class feeling on behalf of some other labor leader who is [[implicated|adj.|/ˈɪmplɪkeɪtɪd/|有牵连的]] in murder. One attitude is as bad as the other, and no worse; in each case the accused is entitled to exact justice; and in neither case is there need of action by others which can be [[construed|v.|/kənˈstruːd/|被理解为，被解释为]] into an expression of sympathy for crime.

> 我们宽容有资本者的恶，与宽容无资本者的恶，其不可容许的程度完全一样，不多也不少。那个因为追究某个托拉斯巨头恶行的努力未能伸张正义而得意洋洋的富人，和那个为另一个涉嫌谋杀的同僚而喧嚷煽动卑劣阶级情绪的所谓劳工领袖一样坏，一点不更坏。两种态度一样坏，谁也不更坏；在两种情形下，被告都有权得到精确的公正；而在两种情形下，都不需要他人采取任何可被解释为同情犯罪的行动。

### 25

It is a [[prime|adj.|/praɪm/|首要的，根本的]] necessity that if the present unrest is to result in permanent good the emotion shall be translated into action, and that the action shall be marked by honesty, sanity, and [[self-restraint|n.|/ˌself rɪˈstreɪnt/|自我克制]]. There is mighty little good in a mere [[spasm|n.|/ˈspæzəm/|一阵发作，短暂冲动]] of reform. The reform that counts is that which comes through steady, continuous growth; violent [[emotionalism|n.|/ɪˈmoʊʃənəlɪzəm/|情绪化，感情用事]] leads to [[exhaustion|n.|/ɪɡˈzɔːstʃən/|耗尽，精疲力竭]].

> 首要之务在于：若要让当前的动荡结出长久的善果，情绪就必须转化为行动，而行动必须以诚实、理智与自我克制为标志。仅仅一阵改良的冲动，几乎毫无益处。真正算数的改良，是经由稳定而持续的生长而来的改良；激烈的情绪化只会导致衰竭。

### 26

It is important to this people to [[grapple|v.|/ˈɡræpl/|应对，设法解决]] with the problems connected with the [[amassing|v.|/əˈmæsɪŋ/|积聚，聚敛]] of enormous fortunes, and the use of those fortunes, both [[corporate|adj.|/ˈkɔːrpərət/|公司的，法人的]] and individual, in business. We should [[discriminate|v.|/dɪˈskrɪmɪneɪt/|区分，辨别]] in the sharpest way between fortunes well won and fortunes ill won; between those gained as an incident to performing great services to the community as a whole and those gained in evil fashion by keeping just within the limits of mere law honesty. Of course, no amount of charity in spending such fortunes in any way [[compensates|v.|/ˈkɑːmpenseɪts/|补偿，抵偿]] for [[misconduct|n.|/ˌmɪsˈkɑːndʌkt/|不当行为，劣行]] in making them.

> 对本国人民而言，重要的是去应对与巨额财富的聚敛及其在商业中的使用（无论公司还是个人）相关的问题。我们应当以最鲜明的方式区分正途所得与邪途所得；区分那些作为为整个社会提供巨大服务之余的附带收益，与那些靠恰好踩在法律诚实边缘的方式以邪恶手段取得的财富。当然，如此得来的财富，无论花出去时多么慷慨，都丝毫不能抵偿赚取它们时的不当行为。

### 27

As a matter of personal [[conviction|n.|/kənˈvɪkʃn/|信念，确信]], and without pretending to discuss the details or [[formulate|v.|/ˈfɔːrmjuleɪt/|制定，构想]] the system, I feel that we shall ultimately have to consider the adoption of some such scheme as that of a [[progressive tax|phr.|/prəˈɡresɪv tæks/|累进税]] on all fortunes, beyond a certain amount, either given in life or devised or [[bequeathed|v.|/bɪˈkwiːðd/|遗赠]] upon death to any individual—a tax so framed as to put it out of the power of the owner of one of these enormous fortunes to hand on more than a certain amount to any one individual; the tax of course, to be imposed by the national and not the state government. Such taxation should, of course, be aimed merely at the [[inheritance|n.|/ɪnˈherɪtəns/|继承，遗产]] or transmission in their entirety of those fortunes swollen beyond all healthy limits. Again, the national government must in some form exercise [[supervision|n.|/ˌsuːpərˈvɪʒn/|监督，监管]] over corporations engaged in [[interstate|adj.|/ˌɪntərˈsteɪt/|州际的]] business—and all large corporations engaged in interstate business—whether by license or otherwise, so as to permit us to deal with the far reaching evils of [[overcapitalization|n.|/ˌoʊvərˌkæpɪtələˈzeɪʃn/|资本过度膨胀]].

> 就个人信念而言，且不打算讨论细节或构想出一套制度，我认为我们最终将不得不考虑采用某种方案，比如对超过一定数额的一切财富——无论是生前赠予，还是在死亡时遗赠或留给任何个人——征收累进税；这种税的设计，要使那些巨额财富的所有者无法把超过一定数额的财产传给任何一个人；这种税当然应由国家政府而非州政府征收。当然，这种课税所针对的，应仅仅是那些膨胀到超出一切健康限度的财富被整体继承或转移的情形。此外，国家政府必须以某种形式，对从事州际业务的公司——以及所有从事州际业务的大公司——行使监督，无论是通过执照还是其他方式，以便使我们能够应对资本过度膨胀所带来的影响深远的弊端。

### 28

This year we are making a beginning in the direction of serious effort to settle some of these economic problems by the railway rate [[legislation|n.|/ˌledʒɪsˈleɪʃn/|立法，法规]]. Such legislation, if so framed, as I am sure it will be, as to secure definite and [[tangible|adj.|/ˈtændʒəbl/|切实的，有形的]] results, will amount to something of itself; and it will amount to a great deal more in so far as it is taken as a first step in the direction of a policy of [[superintendence|n.|/ˌsuːpərɪnˈtendəns/|监督管理]] and control over corporate wealth engaged in interstate commerce; this superintendence and control not to be exercised in a spirit of [[malevolence|n.|/məˈlevələns/|恶意，敌意]] toward the men who have created the wealth, but with the firm purpose both to do justice to them and to see that they in their turn do justice to the public at large.

> 今年，我们正以铁路运价立法为开端，认真着手解决其中一些经济问题。这样的立法，若能制定得——我相信一定能——确保取得明确而切实的成果，其本身就有相当意义；而若把它视为一项对从事州际贸易的公司财富实行监督管理与控制之政策的第一步，其意义就更为重大；这种监督管理与控制的施行，不应出于对创造财富者的敌意，而应抱着坚定的宗旨：既对他们公道，也确保他们反过来对广大公众公道。

### 29

The first [[requisite|n.|/ˈrekwəzɪt/|必要条件，必备素质]] in the public servants who are to deal in this shape with corporations, whether as legislators or as executives, is honesty. This honesty can be no [[respecter of persons|phr.|/rɪˈspektər əv ˈpɜːrsnz/|看人下菜碟者，区别对待者]]. There can be no such thing as [[unilateral|adj.|/ˌjuːnɪˈlætərəl/|单方面的]] honesty. The danger is not really from corrupt corporations; it springs from the [[corruption|n.|/kəˈrʌpʃn/|腐败，贪污]] itself, whether exercised for or against corporations.

> 对于那些将以这种方式处理公司事务的公职人员——无论是立法者还是行政官员——首要的必备素质是诚实。这种诚实不能看人下菜碟。不存在所谓单方面的诚实。危险其实并不来自腐败的公司；危险源于腐败本身，无论这种腐败是替公司办事还是针对公司。

### 30

The eighth [[commandment|n.|/kəˈmændmənt/|诫命，戒律]] reads, “Thou shalt not steal.” It does not read, “Thou shalt not steal from the rich man.” It does not read, “Thou shalt not steal from the poor man.” It reads simply and plainly, “Thou shalt not steal.”

> 第八条诫命说：“不可偷盗。”它没有说：“不可偷富人的东西。”它也没有说：“不可偷穷人的东西。”它说得简单明白：“不可偷盗。”

### 31

No good whatever will come from that [[warped|adj.|/wɔːrpt/|扭曲的，乖戾的]] and [[mock|adj.|/mɑːk/|虚假的，伪装的]] morality which [[denounces|v.|/dɪˈnaʊnsɪz/|谴责，斥责]] the misdeeds of men of wealth and forgets the misdeeds practiced at their expense; which denounces [[bribery|n.|/ˈbraɪbəri/|贿赂]], but blinds itself to [[blackmail|n.|/ˈblækmeɪl/|敲诈，勒索]]; which foams with rage if a corporation secures favors by improper methods, and merely [[leers|v.|/lɪrz/|斜眼而笑，不怀好意地笑]] with hideous [[mirth|n.|/mɜːrθ/|欢笑，嬉笑]] if the corporation is itself wronged.

> 那种扭曲而虚伪的道德不会带来任何好处：它谴责富人的恶行，却忘记以富人为代价所施的恶行；它谴责贿赂，却对敲诈视而不见；如果某家公司以不正当手段谋取好处，它就暴跳如雷；而如果这家公司自己受了冤屈，它却只是带着可憎的嬉笑斜眼旁观。

### 32

The only public servant who can be trusted honestly to protect the rights of the public against the misdeeds of a corporation is that public man who will just as surely protect the corporation itself from wrongful [[aggression|n.|/əˈɡreʃn/|侵犯，侵害]].

> 唯一能够被信任会诚实地保护公众权利、使其免受公司恶行侵害的公职人员，是那种同样会切实保护公司自身免受不法侵犯的人。

### 33

If a public man is willing to yield to popular [[clamor|n.|/ˈklæmər/|喧嚷，鼓噪]] and do wrong to the men of wealth or to rich corporations, it may be set down as certain that if the opportunity comes he will secretly and [[furtively|adv.|/ˈfɜːrtɪvli/|偷偷摸摸地]] do wrong to the public in the interest of a corporation.

> 如果一名公职人员愿意屈从于民众的鼓噪，对富人或富有的公司做出不公之事，那么几乎可以肯定：一旦机会来临，他就会为了某家公司的利益，暗中而鬼祟地做出损害公众之事。

### 34

But in addition to honesty, we need sanity. No honesty will make a public man useful if that man is timid or foolish, if he is a hot-headed [[zealot|n.|/ˈzelət/|狂热分子]] or an [[impracticable|adj.|/ɪmˈpræktɪkəbl/|不切实际的，行不通的]] [[visionary|n.|/ˈvɪʒəneri/|空想家]]. As we strive for reform we find that it is not at all merely the case of a long uphill pull. On the contrary, there is almost as much of [[breeching|n.|/ˈbriːtʃɪŋ/|尻带（挽具部件）]] work as of collar work. To depend only on [[traces|n.|/ˈtreɪsɪz/|挽绳，缰绳]] means that there will soon be a runaway and an upset.

> 但除了诚实，我们还需要理智。一个人若胆怯或愚蠢，若是头脑发热的狂热分子或不切实际的空想家，那么再诚实也无法使他成为有用的公职人员。在争取改良的过程中我们发现，这绝非仅仅是一次漫长的上坡拉车。恰恰相反，其中用尻带承力的活，几乎和用颈轭承力的活一样多。只靠挽绳拉车，意味着不久就会马匹脱缰、车翻人仰。

### 35

The men of wealth who today are trying to prevent the regulation and control of their business in the interest of the public by the proper government authorities will not succeed, in my judgment, in checking the progress of the movement. But if they did succeed they would find that they had [[sown the wind and would surely reap the whirlwind|phr.|/soʊn ðə wɪnd ... riːp ðə ˈwɜːrlwɪnd/|种下风，必收获暴风（自食其果）]], for they would ultimately provoke the violent excesses which accompany a reform coming by [[convulsion|n.|/kənˈvʌlʃn/|剧变，动乱]] instead of by steady and natural growth.

> 今天那些企图阻止正当政府当局为公众利益而对其业务加以规范与控制的富人，依我判断，是无法阻挡这一运动前进的。但即便他们得逞，他们也会发现自己种下的是风、收获的必是暴风；因为他们终将激起的，是那种伴随剧变式改良——而非稳定自然的成长式改良——而来的激烈过火之举。

### 36

On the other hand, the wild preachers of unrest and discontent, the wild agitators against the entire existing order, the men who act crookedly, whether because of [[sinister|adj.|/ˈsɪnɪstər/|邪恶的，居心不良的]] design or from mere puzzle headedness, the men who preach destruction without proposing any [[substitute|n.|/ˈsʌbstɪtuːt/|替代品，替代方案]] for what they intend to destroy, or who propose a substitute which would be far worse than the existing evils—all these men are the most dangerous opponents of real reform. If they get their way they will lead the people into a deeper pit than any into which they could fall under the present system. If they fail to get their way they will still do [[incalculable|adj.|/ɪnˈkælkjələbl/|无法估量的]] harm by provoking the kind of reaction which in its revolt against the senseless evil of their teaching would [[enthrone|v.|/ɪnˈθroʊn/|使登位，使牢固确立]] more securely than ever the evils which their misguided followers believe they are attacking.

> 另一方面，那些狂热鼓吹动荡与不满的人，那些对整个现存秩序发起疯狂攻击的人，那些行事不正的人——无论出于邪恶的图谋还是仅仅因为头脑混乱——那些鼓吹破坏却不为自己意图摧毁之物提出任何替代方案的人，或是提出一种比现存弊端还要糟糕得多的替代方案的人：所有这些人都是真正改良最危险的对手。若他们得逞，他们会把人民带入比现行制度下可能陷入的任何境地都更深的坑中。若他们未能得逞，他们仍会通过挑起某种反作用而造成无法估量的损害；这种反作用在反抗他们那套荒谬邪恶的教条时，会比以往任何时候都更牢固地确立起那些被他们误入歧途的追随者自以为正在攻击的弊端。

### 37

More important than [[aught|pron.|/ɔːt/|任何事物（古语）]] else is the development of the broadest sympathy of man for man. The welfare of the wage worker, the welfare of the [[tiller|n.|/ˈtɪlər/|耕种者，农夫]] of the soil, upon these depend the welfare of the entire country; their good is not to be sought in pulling down others; but their good must be the prime object of all our [[statesmanship|n.|/ˈsteɪtsmənʃɪp/|治国才能，政治智慧]].

> 比任何其他事情都更重要的，是培育人与人之间最广博的同情。工资劳动者的福祉、土地耕种者的福祉，整个国家的福祉都系于此；他们的好处不能靠打倒别人来求得；但他们的好处必须成为我们一切治国方略的首要目标。

### 38

Materially we must strive to secure a broader economic opportunity for all men, so that each shall have a better chance to show the stuff of which he is made. Spiritually and [[ethically|adv.|/ˈeθɪkli/|伦理上，道德上]] we must strive to bring about clean living and right thinking. We appreciate that the things of the body are important; but we appreciate also that the things of the soul are [[immeasurably|adv.|/ɪˈmeʒərəbli/|无可估量地]] more important.

> 在物质上，我们必须努力为所有人争取更广阔的经济机会，使每个人都能有更好的机会展露自己的真才实学。在精神与伦理上，我们必须努力促成洁净的生活与正确的思想。我们懂得身体之事重要；但我们也懂得，灵魂之事重要得无可估量。

### 39

The [[foundation stone|n.|/faʊnˈdeɪʃn stoʊn/|基石，根本]] of national life is, and ever must be, the high individual character of the average citizen.

> 国民生活的基石，现在是、将来也永远是：普通公民高尚的个人品格。
