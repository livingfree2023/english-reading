<section class="para">
  <span class="no">02</span>
  <div class="en">This is a day of national <span class="voc"><span class="w">consecration</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˌkɑːnsɪˈkreɪʃn/ 奉献；神圣化</span></span>, and I am certain that my fellow Americans expect that on my induction into the Presidency I will address them with a <span class="voc"><span class="w">candor</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈkændər/ 坦诚，直率</span></span> and a decision which the present situation of our people <span class="voc"><span class="w">impels</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ɪmˈpelz/ 迫使，驱使</span></span>. Nor need we <span class="voc"><span class="w">shrink</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ʃrɪŋk/ 退缩，畏避</span></span> from honestly facing conditions in our country today.   <div class="zh">这是一个举国奉献的日子，我确信，我的美国同胞们期望我在就任总统之际，以坦诚和决断向他们讲话——我们人民的当前处境正要求如此。现在正是讲真话的时候，讲出全部真话，坦率而勇敢。我们也不必畏缩，而要诚实地面对我国今日的状况。这个伟大的国家将一如既往地坚持下去，将复兴，将繁荣。因此，首先请允许我表明我坚定的信念：我们唯一值得恐惧的，就是恐惧本身——那种无以名之、毫无理智、毫无根据的恐怖，它使人们把退却变为前进所需的种种努力陷于瘫痪。在我国生活的每一个黑暗时刻，坦诚而有力的领导都得到了人民自身的理解与支持，而这种理解与支持是胜利所不可或缺的。我深信，在这危急的日子里，你们会再次把这样的支持给予领导。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">03</span>
  <div class="en">In such a spirit on my part and on yours we face our common difficulties. They concern, thank God, only material things. Values have <span class="voc"><span class="w">shrunken</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ˈʃrʌŋkən/ （shrink 的过去分词）缩减，萎缩</span></span> to fantastic levels; taxes have risen; our ability to pay has fallen; government of all kinds is faced by serious <span class="voc"><span class="w">curtailment</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /kɜːrˈteɪlmənt/ 削减，缩减</span></span> of income; the means of <span class="voc"><span class="w">exchange</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ/ 交换；交易</span></span> are frozen in the currents of trade; the <span class="voc"><span class="w">withered</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈwɪðərd/ 枯萎的，凋零的</span></span> leaves of industrial <span class="voc"><span class="w">enterprise</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈentərpraɪz/ 企业；事业</span></span> lie on every side; farmers find no markets for their <span class="voc"><span class="w">produce</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈproʊduːs/ 农产品</span></span>; the savings of many years in thousands of families are gone.</div>
  <div class="zh">本着我和你们共有的这种精神，我们面对共同的困难。感谢上帝，这些困难只涉及物质方面。各种价值已缩水到离奇的程度；税负上升；我们的支付能力下降；各级政府的收入都面临严重削减；交换手段冻结在贸易的洪流之中；工业企业的枯枝败叶遍地皆是；农民找不到农产品的市场；千千万万家庭多年来的积蓄化为乌有。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">04</span>
  <div class="en">More important, a <span class="voc"><span class="w">host</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /hoʊst/ 大量，许多</span></span> of unemployed citizens face the <span class="voc"><span class="w">grim</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ɡrɪm/ 严峻的，令人沮丧的</span></span> problem of existence, and an equally great number <span class="voc"><span class="w">toil</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /tɔɪl/ 辛苦劳作</span></span> with little return. Only a foolish <span class="voc"><span class="w">optimist</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈɑːptɪmɪst/ 乐观主义者</span></span> can deny the dark realities of the moment.</div>
  <div class="zh">更重要的是，大批失业公民面临生存这一严峻问题，还有同样众多的人劳苦而所得甚微。只有愚蠢的乐观主义者才会否认此刻的黑暗现实。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">05</span>
  <div class="en">Yet our <span class="voc"><span class="w">distress</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /dɪˈstres/ 困苦，痛苦</span></span> comes from no failure of <span class="voc"><span class="w">substance</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈsʌbstəns/ 实质，物质</span></span>. We are stricken by no plague of locusts. Compared with the <span class="voc"><span class="w">perils</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈperəlz/ 危险，危难</span></span> which our forefathers conquered because they believed and were not afraid we have still much to be thankful for. Nature still offers her <span class="voc"><span class="w">bounty</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈbaʊnti/ 慷慨的馈赠，丰饶</span></span> and human efforts have multiplied it. Plenty is at our doorstep, but a generous use of it <span class="voc"><span class="w">languishes</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ˈlæŋɡwɪʃɪz/ 停滞，萎靡不振</span></span> in the very sight of the supply. Primarily this is because rulers of the exchange of mankind's goods have failed through their own stubbornness and their own incompetence, have admitted their failure, and have <span class="voc"><span class="w">abdicated</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ˈæbdɪkeɪtɪd/ 退位，放弃（职责）</span></span>. Practices of the <span class="voc"><span class="w">unscrupulous</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ʌnˈskruːpjələs/ 无所顾忌的，不择手段的</span></span> money changers stand <span class="voc"><span class="w">indicted</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ɪnˈdaɪtɪd/ 控告，起诉</span></span> in the court of public opinion, rejected by the hearts and minds of men.</div>
  <div class="zh">然而我们的困苦并非源于物质的匮乏。我们并未遭受蝗虫之灾。与我们的先辈因为信仰而不畏惧所战胜的种种危难相比，我们仍有太多值得感恩之处。大自然依然赐予丰饶，人类的努力又使之倍增。富足就在我们门前，但就在这供给眼前，对它的慷慨利用却停滞不前。这主要是因为，人类货物交换的统治者们由于自身的顽固和无能而失败了，他们承认了自己的失败，并已引咎退位。那些无所顾忌的货币兑换商的所作所为，已在舆论的法庭上受到指控，被世人的心灵与头脑所唾弃。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">06</span>
  <div class="en">True they have tried, but their efforts have been cast in the pattern of an <span class="voc"><span class="w">outworn</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˌaʊtˈwɔːrn/ 过时的，陈腐的</span></span> tradition. Faced by failure of credit they have proposed only the lending of more money. Stripped of the <span class="voc"><span class="w">lure</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /lʊr/ 诱惑，诱饵</span></span> of profit by which to <span class="voc"><span class="w">induce</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ɪnˈduːs/ 诱使，引导</span></span> our people to follow their false leadership, they have <span class="voc"><span class="w">resorted</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /rɪˈzɔːrtɪd/ 求助于（resort to）</span></span> to <span class="voc"><span class="w">exhortations</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˌeɡzɔːrˈteɪʃnz/ 劝诫，规劝</span></span>, pleading tearfully for restored confidence. They know only the rules of a generation of <span class="voc"><span class="w">self-seekers</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˌself ˈsiːkərz/ 追逐私利者</span></span>. They have no vision, and when there is no vision the people <span class="voc"><span class="w">perish</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /ˈperɪʃ/ 灭亡，消亡</span></span>.</div>
  <div class="zh">诚然他们也曾努力过，但他们的努力不过是套用过时的旧模式。面对信贷的崩溃，他们只提出借出更多的钱。当他们用以引诱人民追随其虚假领导的利润诱饵被剥夺之后，他们便转而求助于劝诫，声泪俱下地恳求恢复信心。他们只懂得一代自私自利者的规矩。他们没有远见，而人民没有远见就会灭亡。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">07</span>
  <div class="en">The money changers have <span class="voc"><span class="w">fled</span><span class="g"><i>v.</i> /fled/ 逃走（flee 的过去式）</span></span> from their high seats in the temple of our civilization. We may now restore that temple to the ancient truths. The measure of the <span class="voc"><span class="w">restoration</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˌrestəˈreɪʃn/ 恢复，复兴</span></span> lies in the <span class="voc"><span class="w">extent</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ɪkˈstent/ 程度，范围</span></span> to which we apply social values more noble than mere <span class="voc"><span class="w">monetary</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈmɑːnɪteri/ 货币的，金钱的</span></span> profit.</div>
  <div class="zh">货币兑换商已从我们文明殿堂的高位上逃走了。我们现在可以把这座殿堂恢复到古老的真理上来。恢复的程度，取决于我们在多大程度上运用比单纯金钱利润更高尚的社会价值。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">08</span>
  <div class="en">Happiness lies not in the mere <span class="voc"><span class="w">possession</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /pəˈzeʃn/ 拥有，占有</span></span> of money; it lies in the joy of achievement, in the <span class="voc"><span class="w">thrill</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /θrɪl/ 激动，兴奋感</span></span> of creative effort. The joy and moral <span class="voc"><span class="w">stimulation</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˌstɪmjuˈleɪʃn/ 激励，激发</span></span> of work no longer must be forgotten in the mad chase of <span class="voc"><span class="w">evanescent</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˌevəˈnesnt/ 转瞬即逝的</span></span> profits.   <div class="zh">幸福不在于仅仅拥有金钱；幸福在于成就的喜悦，在于创造性努力的激动。工作的快乐与道德激励，绝不能再在疯狂追逐转瞬即逝的利润中被遗忘。如果这些黑暗的日子教会我们：我们真正的使命不是受人服侍，而是服侍自己和我们的同胞，那么它们所付出的代价就是值得的。</div>
</section>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">09</span>
  <div class="en">Recognition of the <span class="voc"><span class="w">falsity</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈfɔːlsəti/ 虚假，虚妄</span></span> of material wealth as the standard of success goes hand in hand with the <span class="voc"><span class="w">abandonment</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /əˈbændənmənt/ 抛弃，放弃</span></span> of the false belief that public office and high political position are to be valued only by the standards of pride of place and personal profit; and there must be an end to a conduct in banking and in business which too often has given to a sacred trust the likeness of <span class="voc"><span class="w">callous</span><span class="g"><i>adj.</i> /ˈkæləs/ 冷酷无情的</span></span> and selfish <span class="voc"><span class="w">wrongdoing</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈrɔːŋduːɪŋ/ 恶行，不义之举</span></span>. <section class="para">
  <span class="no">10</span>
  <div class="en">Restoration calls, however, not for changes in <span class="voc"><span class="w">ethics</span><span class="g"><i>n.</i> /ˈeθɪks/ 伦理，道德准则</span></span> alone. This Nation asks for action, and action now.</div>
  <div class="zh">然而，恢复所要求的并不只是伦理上的改变。这个国家要求行动，而且现在就要求行动。</div>

<section class="para">
  <span class="no">11</span>
