1981 年 1 月 20 日 · 华盛顿国会大厦西侧

里根首次就职演说First Inaugural Address — Ronald Reagan

视频Reagan Library 影像 · 在新窗口打开
01
These United States are confronted with an economic affliction of great proportions. We suffer from the longest and one of the worst sustained inflations in our national history.
美利坚合众国正面对规模巨大的经济困境。我们正承受国家历史上持续时间最长、也是最严重的通货膨胀之一。
02
Government is not the solution to our problem; government is the problem. It is my intention to curb the size and influence of the Federal establishment.
政府不是我们问题的答案；政府本身就是问题。我打算遏制联邦机构的规模与影响力。
03
It is my intention to put an end to the government’s practice of borrowing more and spending more and taxing more without end.
我打算终结政府不断借债、不断支出、不断加税的做法。
04
We shall reflect the compassion of our people. We will care for the troubled and protect the weak. We will rally around the disabled, and we will defend the right of every American to a productive life.
我们将体现美国人民的同情心：关怀困顿者，保护弱者，支持残障人士，并捍卫每一个美国人享有充实生活的权利。
原文来源：Ronald Reagan Presidential Library。本页为联邦职务作品的双语精选段落。
